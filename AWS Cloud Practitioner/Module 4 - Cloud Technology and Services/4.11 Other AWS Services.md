# 4.11 Other AWS Services

## 4.11.2 Application integration: connecting parts of an app

| Service | What it does | Exam cue |
|---|---|---|
| **Amazon SQS** (Simple Queue Service) | A **message queue**. One part puts messages in, another takes them out when it's ready. **Decouples** parts of an app | "Decouple", "buffer requests", "queue" |
| **Amazon SNS** (Simple Notification Service) | **Publish and subscribe**: one message sent to **many** subscribers (email, SMS, Lambda, SQS) | "Send notifications", "fan out" |
| **Amazon EventBridge** | An **event bus** that routes events between AWS services and apps, and runs **schedules** | "React to events", "scheduled rules" |
| **AWS Step Functions** | Coordinates steps into a **workflow** (like a flowchart) | "Orchestrate a multi-step workflow" |
| **Amazon MQ** | Managed **ActiveMQ and RabbitMQ** message brokers | "Move an existing message broker" |

**SQS or SNS?** SQS: a message **waits in a queue** for **one** consumer. SNS: a message is **pushed to many** subscribers straight away.

## 4.11.8 Amazon Cognito

**Sign-up and sign-in for your app's users** (not AWS staff).
- **User pools:** user accounts, passwords, MFA, and "Sign in with Google, Apple or Facebook"
- **Identity pools:** give signed-in users temporary AWS credentials

**IAM or Cognito?** **IAM** is for people and services working **in your AWS account**. **Cognito** is for **customers using your app**.

## 4.11.12–4.11.13 Summary and exam tips

- "**Decouple** components" means **SQS**. "**Notify** many subscribers" means **SNS**. "**Event-driven** routing or **schedules**" means **EventBridge**. "**Workflow** of steps" means **Step Functions**.
- "**Contact centre**" means **Amazon Connect**. "**Send emails**" means **SES**.
- "**Virtual desktops**" means **WorkSpaces**.
- "**User sign-in for a web or mobile app**" means **Cognito**.
- "Host a **full-stack web app** quickly" means **Amplify**.
- "Connect **IoT devices**" means **IoT Core**.
