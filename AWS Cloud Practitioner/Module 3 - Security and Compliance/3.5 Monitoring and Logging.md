# 3.5 Monitoring and Logging

## CloudTrail and CloudWatch: the difference

| | **AWS CloudTrail** | **Amazon CloudWatch** |
|---|---|---|
| **Answers** | **Who** did **what**, **when**, and from **where**? | **How** are my resources **performing**? |
| **Records** | **API calls**: every action in the Console, the CLI or an SDK | **Metrics** (like CPU use), **logs** and **events** |
| **Used for** | Auditing, security investigations and compliance | Performance, **alarms**, dashboards and automatic actions |
| **Example** | "Who deleted this S3 bucket yesterday?" | "Tell me when CPU goes above 80%" |

**Memory trick:** CloudTrail leaves a **trail** of user actions. CloudWatch **watches** performance.

## More about each

**CloudTrail**
- **Event history** shows the last **90 days** of management events for free, with no setup needed.
- A **trail** stores events for longer, in an **S3 bucket**.
- CloudTrail Lake lets you query events with SQL.

**CloudWatch**
- **Metrics:** CPU, network traffic, request counts, and your **estimated AWS bill**
- **Alarms:** do something when a metric crosses a threshold (send an email with **SNS**, or scale with Auto Scaling)
- **Logs:** collect application and system logs in one place
- **Dashboards:** graphs of your metrics

**Others**
- **AWS Config:** how resources are **configured**, and the history of changes
- **VPC Flow Logs:** network traffic in and out of your network
- **AWS Health Dashboard:** AWS service problems that could affect you (see Module 5)

## Exam tips (3.5.2)

- "**Audit** who made a change" means **CloudTrail**.
- "**Monitor** CPU or performance" and "**alarm** on a metric" mean **CloudWatch**.
- "Get an email when the **estimated bill** goes over $X" means a **CloudWatch billing alarm** or **AWS Budgets**.
- "Configuration **history** and **compliance**" means **AWS Config**.

## Lab 3.5.3: Enable CloudTrail and view the logs

💰 **Cost:** Event history is free. The **first trail** that records management events is free, and you only pay a little for S3 storage. **Untick KMS encryption** to avoid the KMS key charge.

1. **See the free Event history first:** open **CloudTrail** → **Event history**. You'll see recent actions, like signing in, `CreateUser` from Lab 3.3, and `CreateSecret` from Lab 3.4.
2. Click an event to see **who** did it, **when**, the **source IP** and the details.
3. **Create a trail:** **Trails** → **Create trail**.
   - **Trail name:** `management-events-trail`
   - **Storage location:** **Create new S3 bucket**
   - **Log file SSE-KMS encryption:** **untick** it (to avoid the KMS charge)
4. **Events:** leave **Management events** ticked (Read and Write), then click **Create trail**.
5. After about 15 minutes, open the trail's S3 bucket. The log files are in folders by date.

**🧹 Clean up:** CloudTrail → Trails → select it → **Delete**. Then open **S3**, **empty** the trail's bucket, and **delete** it.

## Lab 3.5.4: Create a CloudWatch alarm (a billing alarm)

💰 **Cost:** 10 alarms are included in the free tier, and email alerts through SNS are free at this level.

1. **Turn on billing alerts:** **Billing and Cost Management** → **Billing preferences** → **Alert preferences** → **Edit** → tick **Receive CloudWatch billing alerts** → **Update**.
2. **Switch Region to US East (N. Virginia) `us-east-1`.** Billing metrics only exist there.
3. Open **CloudWatch** → **Alarms** → **Create alarm** → **Select metric**.
4. **Billing** → **Total Estimated Charge** → tick **USD** → **Select metric**.
5. **Condition:** **Greater than** `5` (US dollars), then **Next**.
6. **Notification:** **Create new topic**, name it `billing-alerts`, enter your **email**, then **Create topic** → **Next**.
7. **Alarm name:** `Bill over 5 dollars`, then **Next** → **Create alarm**.
8. **Check your email** and click **Confirm subscription**, or you won't get the alerts.

**Keep this alarm.** It's a useful safety net while learning.
