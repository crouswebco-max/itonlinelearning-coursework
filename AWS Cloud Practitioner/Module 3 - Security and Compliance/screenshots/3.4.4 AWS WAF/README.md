## Before you save a screenshot

Blur or crop out anything private:
- your **12-digit account ID** and your **email address** (top right of the Console)
- **access keys** and **secret values**, although the lab ones are made up
- anything with your **home address or payment details**

On a Mac: **⌘ + Shift + 4**, then drag over the area. The screenshot saves to your Desktop. Rename it to the matching file name in the table above, and move it into this folder.
