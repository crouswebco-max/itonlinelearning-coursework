# 1.3 Domains and Weightings

The exam is split into four **domains** (topic areas). Each one has a **weighting**: the share of the exam's scored questions that come from it.

| Domain | Weighting | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Cloud Concepts** | **24%** | The benefits of the cloud, the AWS Well-Architected Framework, migrating to the cloud, and cloud economics |
| **2. Security and Compliance** | **30%** | The shared responsibility model, security and compliance, identity and access management (IAM), and security resources |
| **3. Cloud Technology and Services** | **34%** | Deploying and running in AWS, AWS's global infrastructure, and the main compute, database, network, storage, AI/ML and analytics services |
| **4. Billing, Pricing and Support** | **12%** | Pricing models, billing and cost management tools, and support plans and resources |

## What the weightings mean for studying

- **Domain 3 (34%) and Domain 2 (30%) make up almost two-thirds of the exam**, so most study time should go there.
- **Domain 4 (12%) is the smallest**, but it's full of easy marks: the support plans, the pricing models and the cost tools are mostly facts to learn.
- Roughly, out of the **50 scored questions**: about 12 on Cloud Concepts, 15 on Security, 17 on Technology and Services, and 6 on Billing.

## The exam itself

| | |
|---|---|
| **Questions** | 65 in total: 50 scored and 15 unscored (AWS is testing them for future exams, and you can't tell which ones they are) |
| **Question types** | Multiple choice (one right answer) and multiple response (two or more right answers) |
| **Time** | 90 minutes |
| **Passing score** | 700 out of 1000 (the score is scaled) |
| **Wrong answers** | Not penalised, so **never leave a question blank** |
| **Where** | A Pearson VUE test centre, or online from home with a proctor watching |

*Check the [official exam guide](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-cloud-practitioner/) before booking, because AWS updates the details from time to time.*
