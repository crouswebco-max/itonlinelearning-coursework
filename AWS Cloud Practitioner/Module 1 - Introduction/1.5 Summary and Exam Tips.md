# 1.5 Summary and Exam Tips

## Module 1 in five points

1. The **Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02)** is AWS's foundational certification: concepts, services, security and billing, not coding.
2. There are **four domains**: Cloud Concepts (24%), Security and Compliance (30%), Cloud Technology and Services (34%), and Billing, Pricing and Support (12%).
3. **65 questions in 90 minutes**, with 50 scored. You need **700 out of 1000** to pass.
4. Wrong answers aren't penalised, so **answer every question**.
5. A new AWS account should be secured straight away: **MFA on the root user**, a **budget alert**, and a **separate admin user** for daily work.

## Exam tips

- **Read the whole question.** Words like **"MOST cost-effective"**, **"LEAST operational overhead"** and **"MOST secure"** decide which of two correct-looking answers is right.
- **Check how many answers it wants.** "Choose TWO" means two. Getting one of two right still scores zero.
- **Rule out the wrong answers first.** Usually two answers are clearly wrong, which leaves a 50/50.
- **Flag and move on.** Mark hard questions for review and come back at the end, instead of running out of time. That's about 1 minute 20 seconds per question.
- **Watch for made-up service names.** Wrong answers sometimes use services that sound real but don't exist.
- **Think "managed".** AWS usually prefers answers where AWS does more of the work (a managed service) over ones where you run everything yourself.
- **Learn which service does what.** Most questions describe a need, like "store files cheaply for years", and ask which service fits (Amazon S3 Glacier).

## Before booking

- Take at least one **full-length practice exam** under timed conditions.
- Book online (with a proctor) or at a Pearson VUE test centre. For online exams, clear your desk and check your webcam and internet first.
