# 6.6 Final Review and Next Steps

## Am I ready? Checklist

**Knowledge**
- [ ] I can list the **six advantages** of cloud computing
- [ ] I can explain the **shared responsibility model**, including who patches EC2 and who patches RDS
- [ ] I know the **six Well-Architected pillars**
- [ ] I can tell **CloudTrail**, **CloudWatch** and **Config** apart
- [ ] I can tell **GuardDuty**, **Inspector**, **Macie**, **WAF** and **Shield** apart
- [ ] I know which service fits: compute, databases, storage, networking
- [ ] I know the **S3 storage classes**
- [ ] I know the **EC2 pricing models** and when to use each
- [ ] I know the **support plans**, their response times and TAMs
- [ ] I know **Pricing Calculator**, **Cost Explorer** and **Budgets**, and which is for what

**Practice**
- [ ] Scored **80% or more** on the [mock exam](6.5%20Practice%20Activities%20-%20Mock%20Exam.md)
- [ ] Tried the free official practice questions on AWS Skill Builder
- [ ] Did the key labs: IAM user, EC2, S3, DynamoDB and a billing alarm

**Exam day**
- [ ] Exam booked (online or at a test centre)
- [ ] Photo ID ready
- [ ] For online: system test done, quiet room, clear desk
- [ ] Rested, and arriving (or logging in) 30 minutes early

## After you pass

1. **Claim your digital badge** from Credly (AWS emails a link) and add it to **LinkedIn** and your **CV**.
2. **Post on LinkedIn** about passing, with the badge.
3. **Clean up your AWS account:** check **Billing** and delete anything left over from the labs.
4. **Next certifications**, which build on this one:
   - **AWS Certified AI Practitioner**: AI and machine learning basics
   - **AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate**: designing systems on AWS (the most popular next step)
   - **AWS Certified Developer – Associate**: building and deploying apps on AWS, a good fit for a web developer

## Using AWS in my web projects

- **Host a static site** (like my HTML and Tailwind projects): **S3** plus **CloudFront**, or **AWS Amplify**.
- **Host a React app** (like the Money Builder App): **AWS Amplify Hosting** builds and deploys straight from GitHub.
- **Add a back end:** **API Gateway**, **Lambda** and **DynamoDB**, like the mini app in Module 4.
