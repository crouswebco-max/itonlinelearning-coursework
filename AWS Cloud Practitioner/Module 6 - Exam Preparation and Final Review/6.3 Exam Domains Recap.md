# 6.3 Exam Domains Recap: One-Page Cheat Sheet

## Domain 1: Cloud Concepts (24%)

- **Cloud computing:** on-demand IT resources over the internet, with pay-as-you-go pricing.
- **The six advantages:** fixed expense becomes variable, economies of scale, stop guessing capacity, speed and agility, no data centres to run, go global in minutes.
- **Service models:** **IaaS** (EC2) → **PaaS** (Beanstalk) → **SaaS**. Deployment: **cloud**, **hybrid**, **on-premises**.
- **Well-Architected, six pillars:** Operational Excellence, Security, Reliability, Performance Efficiency, Cost Optimisation, Sustainability.
- **CAF, six perspectives:** Business, People, Governance (business side) · Platform, Security, Operations (technical side).
- **The 7 Rs:** Retire, Retain, **Rehost** (lift and shift), Relocate, Repurchase, **Replatform**, **Refactor**.
- **Elasticity** means scaling up **and down** automatically.

## Domain 2: Security and Compliance (30%)

- **Shared responsibility:** AWS handles security **OF** the cloud, and you handle security **IN** the cloud. **You patch EC2. AWS patches RDS.**
- **IAM:** users, groups, **roles** (temporary credentials, for apps and services), and policies. **Least privilege.** **MFA.** Don't use the root user day to day.
- **Root-only tasks:** change the support plan, close the account, change account settings.
- **Compliance:** **Artifact** (reports), **Config** (configuration history and rules), **Audit Manager**.
- **Logging:** **CloudTrail** (who did what) · **CloudWatch** (performance and alarms).
- **Protection:** **WAF** (SQL injection and XSS) · **Shield** (DDoS) · **GuardDuty** (threats) · **Inspector** (vulnerabilities) · **Macie** (sensitive data in S3) · **Security Hub** (all findings in one place).
- **Secrets:** **Secrets Manager** (rotates secrets) · **Parameter Store** (settings) · **KMS** (keys).
- **Security groups:** instance level, stateful, allow only. **NACLs:** subnet level, stateless, allow and deny.

## Domain 3: Cloud Technology and Services (34%)

- **Infrastructure:** Region → **several AZs** (high availability) · edge locations (CloudFront) · Outposts (AWS on-premises).
- **Compute:** **EC2** (servers) · **Lambda** (serverless, 15-minute limit) · **Beanstalk** (PaaS) · **ECS/EKS/Fargate** (containers) · **Lightsail** (simple) · **Auto Scaling** and **ELB**.
- **Databases:** **RDS/Aurora** (relational) · **DynamoDB** (NoSQL) · **ElastiCache** (cache) · **Redshift** (data warehouse).
- **Storage:** **S3** (objects, 11 nines durability) · **EBS** (block disk for one instance) · **EFS** (shared Linux files) · **FSx** (Windows and more) · **Glacier Deep Archive** (cheapest archive).
- **Networking:** **VPC** · **Route 53** (DNS) · **CloudFront** (CDN) · **Direct Connect** (private line) · **VPN** · **Transit Gateway**.
- **Deploying:** **CloudFormation** and **CDK** (infrastructure as code) · **Systems Manager** · CodePipeline, CodeBuild and CodeDeploy.
- **Integration:** **SQS** (queue) · **SNS** (notify) · **EventBridge** (events) · **Step Functions** (workflows).
- **Analytics:** **Athena** (SQL on S3) · **Glue** (ETL) · **Kinesis** (streaming) · **QuickSight** (dashboards).
- **ML:** **SageMaker AI** (build your own) · **Bedrock** (generative AI) · Rekognition, Comprehend, Transcribe, Polly, Lex, Textract and Translate.
- **Other:** **Cognito** (app users' sign-in) · **Amplify** (web and mobile apps) · **Connect** (contact centre) · **SES** (email) · **WorkSpaces** (virtual desktops) · **IoT Core**.

## Domain 4: Billing, Pricing and Support (12%)

- **Pricing:** On-Demand · **Savings Plans** and **Reserved** (up to 72% off, 1 or 3 years) · **Spot** (up to 90% off, can be interrupted) · **Dedicated Hosts** (licences).
- **Data transfer in is free.**
- **Tools:** **Pricing Calculator** (before) · **Cost Explorer** (past) · **Budgets** (alerts) · **Cost and Usage Report** (most detail) · **tags** · **consolidated billing**.
- **Support:** Basic (free) · Developer (email) · **Business** (24/7, < 1 hour, all Trusted Advisor checks) · **Enterprise On-Ramp** (pool of TAMs, < 30 minutes) · **Enterprise** (designated TAM, < 15 minutes).
- **Trusted Advisor:** best-practice checks. **Health Dashboard:** outages affecting you. **re:Post:** community Q&A.
