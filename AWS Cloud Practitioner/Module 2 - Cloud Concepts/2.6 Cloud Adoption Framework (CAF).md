# 2.6 AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (AWS CAF)

Guidance to help an **organisation** move to the cloud successfully. It's not just about technology: it's about people, processes and business goals too.

## The six perspectives

| Perspective | Focus | Who's usually involved |
|---|---|---|
| **Business** | Making sure cloud investments deliver business results | Business managers, finance, strategy |
| **People** | Culture, skills, training and organisational change | HR, staff, leadership |
| **Governance** | Managing risk and getting the most value from the cloud | Chief Information Officer, programme managers |
| **Platform** | Building the cloud platform and architecture | Chief Technology Officer, architects, engineers |
| **Security** | Confidentiality, integrity and availability of data | Chief Information Security Officer, security teams |
| **Operations** | Running and supporting cloud services day to day | IT operations, support managers |

**Tip:** the first three (**Business, People, Governance**) are about **business capabilities**. The last three (**Platform, Security, Operations**) are about **technical capabilities**.

## The four transformation phases

1. **Envision:** show how the cloud will help the business reach its goals.
2. **Align:** find gaps in skills and processes, and get everyone agreed.
3. **Launch:** deliver pilot projects in production and show their value.
4. **Scale:** expand the pilots across the business and grow the benefits.

## The four transformation domains

**Technology**, **Process**, **Organisation** and **Product**: the parts of the business the cloud changes.

## Exam tip

If a question mentions **skills, training, culture or staff**, the answer is the **People** perspective. **Risk and compliance oversight** is **Governance**. **Architecture** is **Platform**.
