# 5.1–5.2 Billing, Pricing and Support: Pricing Models

**Billing, Pricing and Support** is Domain 4 (**12%**). It's the smallest domain, but mostly facts, so it's easy marks.

## The three basics of AWS pricing

1. **Pay as you go:** pay for what you use, with no long contracts.
2. **Save when you commit:** commit to a steady amount of usage for 1 or 3 years for big discounts.
3. **Pay less as you use more:** volume discounts, for example S3 gets cheaper per GB as you store more.

## What you pay for

- **Compute:** by the second or hour (EC2), or per request and millisecond (Lambda)
- **Storage:** per GB per month
- **Data transfer:** **data coming IN to AWS is free**. Data going **OUT** to the internet is charged, and so is data moving between Regions (and usually between AZs)

## EC2 pricing models (heavily tested)

| Model | Discount compared with On-Demand | Commitment | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **On-Demand** | None | None, pay by the second | Short-term, unpredictable workloads, testing, new apps |
| **Savings Plans** | Up to **72%** | Spend a fixed **$ per hour** for **1 or 3 years** | Steady use, with flexibility. **Compute Savings Plans** also cover Fargate and Lambda |
| **Reserved Instances (RIs)** | Up to **72%** | A specific instance type in a Region, for **1 or 3 years** | Steady, predictable workloads (like a database that always runs) |
| **Spot Instances** | Up to **90%** | None, but **AWS can take them back with a 2-minute warning** | Flexible jobs that can be interrupted: batch processing, testing, data analysis |
| **Dedicated Hosts** | None | A whole **physical server** just for you | **Bring-your-own software licences** that are tied to physical cores, and compliance needs |
| **Dedicated Instances** | None | Instances on hardware not shared with other customers | Compliance |
| **Capacity Reservations** | None | Reserve capacity in a specific AZ | Making sure capacity is available when you need it |

### Reserved Instance options
- **Term:** 1 year or 3 years (3 years is cheaper).
- **Payment:** **All upfront** (cheapest), **Partial upfront**, or **No upfront**.
- **Standard RIs** (bigger discount, can't change type) or **Convertible RIs** (smaller discount, can change instance family).

## The AWS Free Tier

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Always free** | Free forever, up to a limit (for example Lambda's 1 million requests a month, and a DynamoDB allowance) |
| **Free trials** | Short trials of certain services (like Security Hub's 30 days) |
| **Free credits for new accounts** | New accounts now get **credits** to spend on many services for a limited time, under a **Free plan** (this replaced the old "12 months free" offers in 2025) |

*Check the [AWS Free Tier page](https://aws.amazon.com/free/) for the current details.*

## 5.2.2 Exam tips

- "**Can be interrupted**, lowest cost" means **Spot**.
- "Steady workload running **all year**" means **Reserved Instances** or **Savings Plans**.
- "Flexible commitment that also covers **Lambda and Fargate**" means **Compute Savings Plans**.
- "**Short-term, unpredictable**, no commitment" means **On-Demand**.
- "**Server-bound software licences**" means **Dedicated Hosts**.
- Data transfer **in** is **free**.
