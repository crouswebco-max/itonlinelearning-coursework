# 5.4 AWS Support Plans

| | **Basic** | **Developer** | **Business** | **Enterprise On-Ramp** | **Enterprise** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Cost** | **Free** | From $29 a month | From $100 a month | From $5,500 a month | From $15,000 a month |
| **For** | Everyone | **Experimenting and testing** | **Production** workloads | Business-critical workloads | **Mission-critical** workloads |
| **Technical support** | ❌ (billing and account help only) | Email, business hours | **24/7 phone, email and chat** | 24/7 phone, email and chat | 24/7 phone, email and chat |
| **Fastest response** | – | < 12 business hours (system impaired) | **< 1 hour** (production system down) | **< 30 minutes** (business-critical system down) | **< 15 minutes** (business-critical system down) |
| **Trusted Advisor** | Core checks | Core checks | **All checks** | All checks | All checks |
| **Technical Account Manager (TAM)** | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | A **pool** of TAMs | A **designated** TAM |
| **Concierge support team** (billing and account experts) | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **AWS Health API** | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

*Prices are minimums, and the rest is charged as a percentage of your AWS bill. AWS updates its support plans from time to time, so check the [AWS Support plans page](https://aws.amazon.com/premiumsupport/plans/) before the exam.*

## What everyone gets (Basic)

- Customer service for **billing and account** questions
- Documentation, whitepapers and **AWS re:Post** (the community Q&A)
- **AWS Health Dashboard**
- **Trusted Advisor core checks** (the key security checks and service limits)

## Exam tips

- "**Cheapest** plan with **technical** support" means **Developer**.
- "**24/7 phone** support" and a "**production** workload" mean at least **Business**.
- "**Less than 1 hour** response for a production system that's down" means **Business**.
- "**Less than 15 minutes**" and a "**designated TAM**" mean **Enterprise**.
- "A **pool** of TAMs" and "less than 30 minutes" mean **Enterprise On-Ramp**.
- "**All** Trusted Advisor checks" means **Business or higher**.
- Only the **root user** can **change the support plan**.
