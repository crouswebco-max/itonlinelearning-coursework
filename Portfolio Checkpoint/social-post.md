## LinkedIn

Just finished my portfolio site, built with Tailwind CSS.

Four pages — home, work, gallery and contact — laid out with Tailwind's utility classes, with my own brand colours added through an @theme block. It's a grid on a computer and a single column on a phone, and it's live on GitHub Pages.

The part that took longest was the header: fitting a logo, a menu and a button onto a phone screen without it looking cramped. The menu now wraps onto its own row below about 640px.

I wrote up how I built it here: https://crouswebco-max.github.io/itonlinelearning-coursework/Portfolio%20Checkpoint/blog.html
