Just finished my portfolio site, built with Tailwind CSS.

Four pages — home, work, gallery and contact — laid out with Tailwind's utility classes, with my own brand colours added through an `@theme` block. It's a grid on a computer and a single column on a phone, and it's live on GitHub Pages.

The part that took the longest was the header: fitting a logo, a menu and a button onto a phone screen without it looking cramped. The menu now wraps onto its own row below about 640px.

This is part of my web development studies with ITonlinelearning, alongside the client work I do at Crous WebCo.
