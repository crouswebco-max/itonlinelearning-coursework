About Us

Elegant Yachting Tours began with one small sailing boat and a love of the open sea. Today our sailing yachts take guests to quiet bays, sandy beaches and hidden coves that most visitors never get to see.

Why sail with us?

  • Experienced, friendly skippers on every trip
  • Small groups, so every guest gets a great view
  • Safety equipment and a full briefing before we set sail
  • Snorkelling gear, drinks and snacks included
