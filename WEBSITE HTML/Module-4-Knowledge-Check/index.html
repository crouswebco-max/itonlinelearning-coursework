Discover the ocean's mysteries

Leave the shore behind and let the wind carry you somewhere new. Aboard our sailing yachts, you'll drop anchor in hidden coves, swim in water so clear you can see the seabed, and watch dolphins race alongside the bow. Our experienced skippers take care of every detail, so whether it's your first time on deck or your hundredth voyage, all you need to do is breathe in the salt air and enjoy the view. And as the sun sinks into the open sea, you'll see why our guests keep coming back.

Why sail with us?

Every trip is led by a skipper who knows these waters by heart. We keep our groups small, so there's always room to stretch out on deck, and we're happy to slow down for a swim stop or a closer look at the wildlife.

Where we sail

From sheltered bays with calm turquoise water to the open sea where the wind fills the sails, every route is planned around the weather and the season. You'll visit quiet beaches and hidden coves that are hard to reach by land.

What's included

Life jackets, snorkelling gear, fresh drinks and light snacks come with every tour. Just bring sunscreen, a towel and your camera, and we'll take care of the rest.
